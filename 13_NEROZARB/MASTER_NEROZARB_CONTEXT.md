### 5.1 The 6-Level Dopamine Ladder [141, 483]
Guiding a cold prospect from a complete stranger to a hyper-committed brand advocate by moving them up six distinct neurological rungs:
1. **Stimulation (Level 1):** Hacking the bottoms-up visual brain. Triggered within 200ms using raw color contrast, high-speed movement, and rapid scale changes to force the optic nerve to pause the scroll [141, 483].
2. **Captivation (Level 2):** Opening the Curiosity Loop. Triggered by presenting an open-ended, non-obvious question in their mind that they must resolve (The "Mental Itch") [141, 483].
3. **Anticipation (Level 3):** The predictive guessing game. The viewer begins calculating what the answer might be. You feed them highly structured, escalating details to fuel this prediction [141, 483].
4. **Validation (Level 4):** Closing the loop. Delivering the non-obvious, high-value solution or shocking story climax that exceeds their expectation, releasing a massive wave of chemical relief [141, 483].
5. **Affection (Level 5):** Shifting focus from the message to the messenger. The viewer develops affinity for the creator's raw charisma, sets, lighting, tone, and authentic visual taste [141, 483].
6. **Revelation (Level 6):** The Pavlovian Trigger. The viewer realizes that seeing your profile icon or name is a guaranteed shortcut to high-value solutions. Their brain releases dopamine *simply by seeing your profile icon*, cementing them as a super fan [141, 483].

### 10.1 Core Mathematical Formulas

*   **Video Hook Rate (Scroll-Stop) [20]:**
    $$\text{Hook Rate (\%)} = \left( \frac{\text{3-Second Video Views}}{\text{Total Impressions}} \right) \times 100$$
*   **Blended Conversion Rate (CVR) [57]:**
    $$\text{CVR (\%)} = \left( \frac{\text{Total Purchases}}{\text{Total Outbound Clicks}} \right) \times 100$$
*   **Marketing Efficiency Ratio (MER) [41, 57]:**
    $$\text{MER} = \frac{\text{Total Business Revenue}}{\text{Total Paid Ad Spend}}$$
*   **Lifetime Value (Gross Profit) [57]:**
    $$\text{LTV-GP} = \text{Lifetime Revenue per Customer} \times \text{Gross Profit Margin (\%)}$$
*   **Floor Price (B2B Productized Service) [173, 528]:**
    $$\text{Floor Price} = \text{Internal Delivery Cost} \times 2$$
*   **Value Price (Weiss & Baker Formula) [173, 528]:**
    $$\text{Value Price} \approx U \times F \times (1 - D)$$
    *Where $U$ is the estimated client upside, $F$ is the standard fair-share percentage (10-20%), and $D$ is the uncertainty discount (30-60%) [173, 528].*
