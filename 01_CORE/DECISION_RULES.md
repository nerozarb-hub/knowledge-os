## SECTION 1: MARKETING & AD STRATEGY DECISION RULES

### RULE 1.1: SKU-Count Design Selector & Campaign Architecture Routing
* **IF** the brand product catalog contains **greater than 50 SKUs** [NB-001, MASTER_MARKETING_OS, MASTER_NEROZARB_CONTEXT]:
  * **THEN** bypass single-product UGC development. Launch a dedicated **Catalog Sales campaign** with enriched Marpipe style frames, dynamic price callouts, and 4-grid collage statics to liquidate surplus stock, drive conversions, and prevent creative fatigue [NB-001].
* **IF** the brand catalog contains **less than 10 SKUs** [NB-001, MASTER_MARKETING_OS, MASTER_NEROZARB_CONTEXT]:
  * **THEN** focus 100% of creative operations on high-fidelity Founder storytelling, direct-response UGC, and "breakup letter" static concepts to build extreme niche trust and customer empathy [NB-001, MASTER_MARKETING_OS].
* **BECAUSE** large SKU catalogs suffer from high operational drag and immediate creative fatigue if restricted to single products, whereas small SKU portfolios lack scale and must establish deep emotional authority and direct-response mechanisms to succeed [NB-001, MASTER_MARKETING_OS].
* **CHECK** verify exact catalog SKU counts in Meta Commerce Manager before setting up the campaign; ensure SKU feed data is fully synchronized [MASTER_MARKETING_OS].
* **DO NOT** mix dynamic catalog sales ads with single-product UGC creatives in the same ad sets, as this disrupts Meta's bidding algorithms and triggers learning resets [NB-001, MASTER_PAID_ADS_OS].
* **ESCALATE WHEN** the brand SKU count falls between 10 and 50 (the "No Man's Land" zone), requiring a customized hybrid allocation audit by the Chief Media Buyer [MASTER_MARKETING_OS].
* **Sources:** [NB-001], [MASTER_MARKETING_OS], [MASTER_NEROZARB_CONTEXT], [MASTER_PAID_ADS_OS]

### RULE 1.2: Creative Sandbox Testing to Scaling CBO Allocation (Post-ID Graduation)
* **IF** a new ad creative exceeds the historical target ROAS/CPA in the ABO testing sandbox over a rolling 7-day window [NB-001, MASTER_MARKETING_OS, MASTER_NEROZARB_CONTEXT]:
  * **THEN** graduate the asset by duplicating the exact **post-ID** directly into the primary broad scaling CBO or Advantage+ Shopping Campaign (ASC) [NB-001, NB-003].
* **BECAUSE** duplicating the post-ID preserves all accumulated social proof (likes, comments, shares, views), which lowers CPMr and stabilizes performance in higher-budget auctions [NB-001, MASTER_MARKETING_OS, MASTER_PAID_ADS_OS].
* **CHECK** confirm that the post-ID is copied exactly without altering any copy, headlines, or visual files; verify the original ABO ad set had at least 3 to 5 days of consistent conversion volume [NB-001, MASTER_PAID_ADS_OS].
* **DO NOT** edit the creative file, headline, or copy in the scaled campaign, as any change resets the post-ID, wipes out social proof, and triggers Meta's learning phase [NB-001, NB-003, MASTER_PAID_ADS_OS].
* **ESCALATE WHEN** post-ID duplication fails due to Meta API errors, or when the scaled CBO's blended CAC spikes >50% above target within the first 48 hours of graduation [MASTER_PAID_ADS_OS].
* **Sources:** [NB-001], [NB-003], [MASTER_MARKETING_OS], [MASTER_NEROZARB_CONTEXT], [MASTER_PAID_ADS_OS]

### RULE 1.3: Creative Fatigue Recovery & CPMr Spike Control
* **IF** campaign frequency at the ad unit level rises **above 2.0 within a 30-day window** [NB-001], OR campaign CPMr spikes by **greater than 25% week-over-week** [NB-003], OR CTR drops **below 1.5%** [NB-003], OR blended ROAS declines for **3 consecutive days** [MASTER_AGENT_OPERATING_SYSTEM]:
  * **THEN** do NOT adjust manual bid caps or target audience interests [NB-003, MASTER_MARKETING_OS].
  * **AND** trigger the **Hook Refurbishing Protocol**: Swap out only the visual hook and first 3 seconds of the asset to reset its similarity score in Meta's algorithm [NB-001, MASTER_MARKETING_OS, MASTER_PAID_ADS_OS].
  * **AND** inject a conceptually diverse batch of creatives using the **60/30/10 Budget Allocation Rule** (60% to evergreen concepts, 30% to conceptual tests, 10% to seasonal/promos) [NB-003, MASTER_MARKETING_OS, MASTER_PAID_ADS_OS].
* **BECAUSE** adjusting targeting or bid caps triggers learning resets and increases auction overlap. Similarity scores in Meta's Andromeda algorithm clump identical creatives under the same Entity ID; swapping only the hook resets the asset's uniqueness score, extending its lifecycle [NB-001, NB-003, MASTER_PAID_ADS_OS].
* **CHECK** audit the Meta Creative Similarity Report to identify similarity clumping; ensure the new hooks are distinct in color palette and movement pacing [MASTER_AD_CREATIVE_OS, MASTER_PAID_ADS_OS].
* **DO NOT** reactively alter bid caps, double down on "lazy volume" minor variants, or shut down campaigns without checking the blended MER first [NB-001, NB-003, MASTER_PAID_ADS_OS].
* **ESCALATE WHEN** Hook Refurbishing fails to stabilize CTR or CPMr after three consecutive iterations within 14 days, indicating systemic account-level fatigue [MASTER_MARKETING_OS].
* **Sources:** [NB-001], [NB-003], [MASTER_AD_CREATIVE_OS], [MASTER_MARKETING_OS], [MASTER_PAID_ADS_OS], [MASTER_AGENT_OPERATING_SYSTEM]

### RULE 1.4: Niche Expansion (Niche Down vs. Go Broad)
* **IF** the brand has **under 10,000 followers** and zero validated customer feedback loops [NB-011, MASTER_MARKETING_OS]:
  * **THEN** focus 100% of operations on **micro-niching** using the "Rule of One" (one problem, one solution, one specific audience) to build initial authority [NB-011, MASTER_MARKETING_OS].
* **IF** the brand has **crossed 50,000 followers**, built a consistent inbound pipeline, and wants to expand top-of-funnel reach [NB-011, MASTER_MARKETING_OS]:
  * **THEN** "niche up" and integrate broader total addressable market (TAM) content (mindset, work culture, remote work) into the secondary content pillars [NB-011, MASTER_MARKETING_OS].
* **BECAUSE** micro-scale brands lack the conversion data and brand capital to convert broad interests, whereas larger, established brands experience audience stagnation if confined to a hyper-specific niche [NB-011, MASTER_MARKETING_OS].
* **CHECK** verify that audience growth and monthly sales are consistent before attempting to niche up; review the "Trust Deficit" metrics in qualitative reports [MASTER_MARKETING_OS].
* **DO NOT** dilute focus with generalist, broad-market content when under the 10,000 follower threshold, as this triggers the "Variety Show" trap [MASTER_MARKETING_OS, MASTER_CONTENT_OS].
* **ESCALATE WHEN** high-reach broad content fails to convert to middle-funnel leads, causing blended CAC to spike by 40% [MASTER_MARKETING_OS].

## SECTION 2: SALES & OUTBOUND DECISION RULES

### RULE 2.1: B2B Deal Value & Complexity Mapping (ACV-Based Framework Selector)
* **IF** estimated Annual Contract Value (ACV) is **<$20K** (SMB / short cycle) [NB-014, MASTER_OUTBOUND_OS, MASTER_MARKETING_OS]:
  * **THEN** deploy **BANT** (Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline) for rapid inbound lead filtering and pair with Sandler's upfront contracts to protect rep time [NB-014, MASTER_OUTBOUND_OS].
* **IF** estimated ACV is **$20K–$100K** (Mid-Market) [NB-014, MASTER_OUTBOUND_OS, MASTER_MARKETING_OS]:
  * **THEN** deploy Neil Rackham's **SPIN Selling** sequence for implication-focused discovery to progressively expand problem awareness [NB-014, MASTER_OUTBOUND_OS].
* **IF** estimated ACV is **$100K–$250K** (High Mid-Market SaaS) [NB-014, MASTER_OUTBOUND_OS, MASTER_MARKETING_OS]:
  * **THEN** deploy **SPICED** (Situation, Pain, Impact, Critical Event, Decision) to align GTM teams [NB-014, MASTER_OUTBOUND_OS].
* **IF** estimated ACV is **>$250K+** (Strategic Enterprise) [NB-014, MASTER_OUTBOUND_OS, MASTER_MARKETING_OS]:
  * **THEN** standardize on **MEDDPICC** with rigorous stakeholder matrices to navigate complex procurement and technical hurdles [NB-014, MASTER_OUTBOUND_OS].
* **BECAUSE** lower deal values require high transactional velocity and zero administrative drag to protect margins, whereas six-figure deals feature complex buying committees and require multi-stakeholder diagnostic alignment to avoid deal stagnation [NB-014, MASTER_OUTBOUND_OS, MASTER_SALES_OS].
* **CHECK** calculate estimated target ACV using corporate research before the initial discovery call; confirm the appropriate playbook is loaded [MASTER_SALES_OS].
* **DO NOT** waste strategic enterprise frameworks (e.g., MEDDPICC matrices) on sub-$20k prospects, and never use high-velocity BANT filtering on enterprise accounts [MASTER_SALES_OS].
* **ESCALATE WHEN** an SMB deal balloons in complexity, requiring multiple custom presentations, or an enterprise deal requests an expedited 48-hour signature [MASTER_SALES_OS].

### RULE 2.2: B2B Pricing, Value Framing & Offer Selection
* **IF** pricing a productized B2B SaaS or consulting engagement [NB-013, MASTER_MARKETING_OS]:
  * **THEN** calculate the value-based proposal price using the formula:
    $$Value Price \\approx U \\times F \\times (1 - D)$$
    Where:
    * $U$ (Upside): Estimated financial gain/savings for the client (e.g., $100,000) [NB-013].
    * $F$ (Fair-share %): Standard 10%–20% value captured by the vendor [NB-013].
    * $D$ (Uncertainty Discount %): Standard 30%–60% discount for perceived execution risk [NB-013].
  * **AND** ensure the final proposal price sits comfortably above the Internal Cost Floor:
    $$Floor Price = Internal Cost \\times 2$$ [NB-013]
* **BECAUSE** cost-plus-margin structures cap margins and fail to align price with the client's commercial risk, whereas value-based pricing matches vendor incentives with actual upside while discounting for execution uncertainty [NB-013, MASTER_MARKETING_OS].
* **CHECK** validate the estimated financial gain $U$ with client-specific metrics before writing the proposal; ensure $F$ and $D$ are explicitly documented [MASTER_MARKETING_OS].
* **DO NOT** offer performance-only pricing where upside $U$ cannot be verified, or drop proposal pricing below the Internal Cost Floor [NB-013, MASTER_OUTBOUND_OS].
* **ESCALATE WHEN** a prospect demands a performance-only structure, or when estimated financial upside $U$ is negative or cannot be quantified [MASTER_OUTBOUND_OS].
* **Sources:** [NB-013], [MASTER_MARKETING_OS], [MASTER_OUTBOUND_OS]

### RULE 2.3: High-Ticket Objection Handling and "Cold Water" Reversal

  * **AND** execute the **"Cold Water" Reversal**: Hold the price anchor firmly, but modify the terms by removing high-value deliverables proportionately [NB-015, NB-017].
* **BECAUSE** arbitrary discounting signals desperate margins and destroys high-status positioning, whereas reducing deliverables preserves the integrity of the pricing model and teaches the client that every output has a cost [NB-015, NB-017, MASTER_SALES_OS].
* **CHECK** ensure the verbatim script is memorized and delivered with calm, neutral tone: *"I drank some cold water and thought about this. I cannot do the full project for 80,000 PKR because I refuse to compromise on quality... If you want the complete ecosystem that actually plugs your ad spend leak, the investment is 110,000 PKR."* [NB-017]
* **DO NOT** argue, defend, reactively discount, or walk away without offering the step-down deliverables card [NB-017, MASTER_SALES_OS].
* **ESCALATE WHEN** dealing with enterprise accounts requiring procurement-approved volume discounts or master service agreement (MSA) negotiations [MASTER_SALES_OS].
* **Sources:** [NB-015], [NB-016], [NB-017], [MASTER_CONTENT_PSYCHOLOGY_OS], [MASTER_SALES_OS]

    * **Sophistication Level 3 (Skeptical Market):** Suppress simple benefit claims. Reframe copy entirely around your **Unique Mechanism** (the *how* behind the *what*) using **long-form, educational copy** [NB-009].

* **BECAUSE** saturated markets ignore simple benefit claims and reject mechanisms once they are widespread, requiring a deep shift toward mechanism proof or narrative/identity alignment to bypass skepticism [NB-009, NB-013, MASTER_CONTENT_PSYCHOLOGY_OS].
* **CHECK** verify the market sophistication level before drafting copy; ensure long-form copy contains readability subheadings [MASTER_COPYWRITING_OS].

### RULE 3.2: Direct-Response Copywriting Constraints (The 15/3 Constraint)

  * **AND** ensure the readability score hits a 3rd-grade reading level (Flesch Ease 60-80) [NB-004, NB-009, MASTER_COPYWRITING_OS].
  * **AND** keep B2B cold emails strictly under the **100-word limit** [MASTER_OUTBOUND_OS].

* **CHECK** run a word counter and Flesch readability tool on drafted copy; check for and remove banned corporate words [NB-011, NB-012].
* **DO NOT** wrap multiple ideas in single compound sentences, or create blocks of text that look like academic essays on mobile [NB-012, MASTER_COPYWRITING_OS].

## SECTION 4: COMPUTATIONAL CINEMATOGRAPHY & VISUAL PRODUCTION RULES

### RULE 4.1: Vertical Axis Angle Selection (Power Mapping)
* **IF** the subject or product represents aspiration, heroism, or dominant authority [NB-022, NB-027, MASTER_AI_IMAGE_GENERATION_OS]:
  * **THEN** route to a **Low Angle / Worm's-Eye View** (camera below subject's eye-line, pointing upward) [NB-022, NB-027]. Paired with a slow orbit, this elevates the subject to heroic status [MASTER_AI_IMAGE_GENERATION_OS].
* **IF** the subject is vulnerable, observed, monitored, or judged [NB-022, NB-027, MASTER_AI_IMAGE_GENERATION_OS]:
  * **THEN** route to a **High Angle / Bird's-Eye View** (camera above subject, pointing downward) [NB-022, NB-027, MASTER_AI_IMAGE_GENERATION_OS].
  * **AND** enforce the **Suspense Overhead Rule**: If pointing straight down along the vertical axis, it creates intense suspense by concealing horizontal depth, preventing the viewer from seeing what enters the frame next [MASTER_AI_IMAGE_GENERATION_OS].
* **IF** natural, low-tension peer relationship or documentary honesty is required [NB-022, NB-027, MASTER_AI_IMAGE_GENERATION_OS]:
  * **THEN** route to **Eye Level** perspective (camera placed at the exact physical level of eyes, horizon line centered) [NB-022, NB-027, MASTER_AI_IMAGE_GENERATION_OS].
* **IF** psychological unease, moral disorientation, or systemic tension is required [NB-022, NB-027, MASTER_AI_IMAGE_GENERATION_OS]:
  * **THEN** route to **Dutch Angle / Canted Horizon** (camera tilted on its horizontal axis) [NB-022, NB-027, MASTER_AI_IMAGE_GENERATION_OS].
  * **AND** ensure there are clear vertical or horizontal reference lines (trees, buildings) in the background to ground the tilt, and the tilt must be dramatic (15+ degrees) to look highly intentional [MASTER_AI_IMAGE_GENERATION_OS].
* **BECAUSE** camera angles manipulate spatial geometry and trigger subconscious power-dynamic mappings based on human evolutionary psychology [NB-022, MASTER_VISUAL_PRODUCTION_OS].
* **CHECK** verify the spatial coordinates and angle values in the visual brief or JSON director's script before rendering [MASTER_VISUAL_PRODUCTION_OS].
* **DO NOT** mix low angles and high angles arbitrarily within the same sequence without a narrative transition [MASTER_VISUAL_PRODUCTION_OS].
* **ESCALATE WHEN** a luxury client demands flat eye-level angles for a hero shot, contradicting the aspiration low-angle rule [MASTER_VISUAL_PRODUCTION_OS].
* **Sources:** [NB-022], [NB-027], [MASTER_AGENT_OPERATING_SYSTEM], [MASTER_AI_IMAGE_GENERATION_OS], [MASTER_AI_VIDEO_GENERATION_OS], [MASTER_VISUAL_PRODUCTION_OS]

### RULE 4.2: Camera Movement Trajectory (Emotional Physics)
* **IF** the narrative goal is to draw the viewer inward, force concentration, highlight internal conflict, or reveal realizations [NB-022, NB-027, MASTER_AI_IMAGE_GENERATION_OS]:
  * **THEN** route motion prompts to **Dolly Push-In (Dolly In)** (camera physically moves forward along the Z-axis, shifting from wide to tight) [NB-022, NB-027, MASTER_AI_IMAGE_GENERATION_OS].
* **IF** the goal is to convey isolation, scale reveal, helplessness, or gradually reveal environment context [NB-022, NB-027, MASTER_AI_IMAGE_GENERATION_OS]:
  * **THEN** route motion prompts to **Dolly Pull-Out (Dolly Out)** (camera physically moves backward along the Z-axis, expanding from tight to wide) [NB-022, NB-027, MASTER_AI_IMAGE_GENERATION_OS].
* **IF** dimensional depth separation, three-dimensional spatial parallax, or dynamic showcase of a stationary subject is required [NB-022, NB-027, MASTER_AI_IMAGE_GENERATION_OS]:
  * **THEN** route motion prompts to **Parallax Slide / Slow Orbit** (moving laterally while keeping the lens focused and centered on the subject) [NB-022, NB-027, MASTER_AI_IMAGE_GENERATION_OS].
* **BECAUSE** camera movement mimics human physical navigation; pushing in accelerates psychological focus, pulling out detaches the viewer, and panning/sliding creates spatial depth [NB-022, MASTER_VISUAL_PRODUCTION_OS].
* **CHECK** verify spatial depth variables and focal point locking in AI video generation prompts [MASTER_AI_VIDEO_GENERATION_OS].
* **DO NOT** use camera pans when depth parallax is needed, and never mix random movement axes in high-end product sequences [MASTER_AI_VIDEO_GENERATION_OS, MASTER_VISUAL_PRODUCTION_OS].
* **ESCALATE WHEN** video rendering engines introduce severe spatial distortion or warped perspectives during Z-axis movements [MASTER_AI_VIDEO_GENERATION_OS].
* **Sources:** [NB-022], [NB-027], [MASTER_AGENT_OPERATING_SYSTEM], [MASTER_AI_IMAGE_GENERATION_OS], [MASTER_AI_VIDEO_GENERATION_OS], [MASTER_VISUAL_PRODUCTION_OS]

### RULE 4.3: Anti-Plastic Shading & Physics-Based Rendering Control
* **IF** prompting high-fidelity human portraits or premium fabric/product assets [NB-022, NB-027, MASTER_AI_IMAGE_GENERATION_OS]:
  * **THEN** inject positive physical parameters: **Subsurface Scattering (SSS)** for waxy skin prevention (`micro-pore skin texture, natural oil variations, vellus hair peach fuzz along facial contours`); **Marschner Hair Model** for realistic individual hair strands; **Anisotropic Highlights** for luxury fabrics [MASTER_AI_IMAGE_GENERATION_OS, MASTER_AI_VIDEO_GENERATION_OS, MASTER_VISUAL_PRODUCTION_OS].
  * **AND** always generate character bases on solid neutral gray backdrops (`#808080`) to lock clean face values and prevent blowouts [MASTER_AI_IMAGE_GENERATION_OS].
* **BECAUSE** standard diffusion models suffer from waxy latent-space bias and default to smooth, waxy surfaces; forcing precise physical shading parameters breaks this bias and emulates real camera behavior [MASTER_AI_IMAGE_GENERATION_OS, MASTER_AI_VIDEO_GENERATION_OS].
* **CHECK** run a visual check on generated assets; ensure zero "AI sheen" is present [MASTER_VISUAL_PRODUCTION_OS].
* **DO NOT** generate on pure white seamless backdrops (which causes plastic face traps due to light bleed) [MASTER_AI_IMAGE_GENERATION_OS].
* **ESCALATE WHEN** diffusion models continue to produce waxy skin despite positive prompt overrides, indicating base model fine-tuning issues [MASTER_AI_IMAGE_GENERATION_OS].
* **Sources:** [NB-022], [NB-027], [MASTER_AI_IMAGE_GENERATION_OS], [MASTER_AI_VIDEO_GENERATION_OS], [MASTER_VISUAL_PRODUCTION_OS]

### RULE 4.4: Cinematic Physics (180-Degree Shutter & 30-Degree Rules)
* **IF** generating motion video assets [MASTER_AI_VIDEO_GENERATION_OS, MASTER_VISUAL_PRODUCTION_OS]:
  * **THEN** enforce the **180-Degree Shutter Rule** (physics-based motion blur relative to frame rate) [MASTER_AI_VIDEO_GENERATION_OS].
  * **AND** ensure a minimum **30-degree camera angle shift** between consecutive cuts of the same subject [MASTER_AI_VIDEO_GENERATION_OS].
* **BECAUSE** standard AI video generation without motion blur looks unnatural, while cutting with less than a 30-degree angle shift causes a jarring "jump cut" that breaks cognitive continuity [MASTER_AI_VIDEO_GENERATION_OS, MASTER_VISUAL_PRODUCTION_OS].
* **CHECK** verify frame rate and motion blur parameters in export settings; ensure storyboard cuts have distinct spatial shifts [MASTER_VISUAL_PRODUCTION_OS].
* **DO NOT** make flat cuts with identical focal lengths and angles [MASTER_VISUAL_PRODUCTION_OS].
* **ESCALATE WHEN** frame interpolation tools introduce severe geometric warping when calculating motion blur [MASTER_AI_VIDEO_GENERATION_OS].
* **Sources:** [MASTER_AI_VIDEO_GENERATION_OS], [MASTER_VISUAL_PRODUCTION_OS]
