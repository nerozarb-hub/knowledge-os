## 4. NEVER

### 4.1 RESEARCH
*   **Manual Lead Churning [Fact/Principle]**: Never assemble outbound marketing or sales lists using manual, unstructured Google search scraping or arbitrary spreadsheet entry without passing them through the NERO Programmatic Sifting Pipeline [MASTER_AGENT_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md SOP 5.1].

### 4.2 STRATEGY
*   **Ad Set Fragmentation [Fact/Principle]**: Never split ad campaign budgets across dozens of small ad sets. Campaign fragmentation starves Meta's Andromeda engine, preventing the system from clearing the 50-conversions-per-week learning threshold [MASTER_AGENT_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md 2.2].

### 4.3 EXECUTION
*   **Prohibited Linguistic Vocabulary [Fact/Principle]**: Never use banned buzzwords under any circumstances: *leverage, ecosystem, navigate, landscape, delve, testament, pivotal, transformative, groundbreaking, vibrant, foster, showcase, passionate, next-level, synergy, comprehensive* [MASTER_AGENT_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md SOP 5.3].
*   **Conversational Fillers [Fact/Principle]**: Never start or end outputs with conversational preambles or polite filler ("Great question," "Certainly," "Absolutely," "I hope this helps," "Let me know if you need anything else") [MASTER_AGENT_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md SOP 5.3].

### 4.4 PROMPTING
*   **Optical Physics Contradiction [Fact/Principle]**: Never pair wide-angle focal lengths with ultra-shallow depth of field aperture commands (e.g., "15mm shot with f/1.2 shallow background bokeh") [MASTER_VISUAL_PRODUCTION_OS.md 258]. This represents an optical impossibility that breaks spatial neural mapping [MASTER_VISUAL_PRODUCTION_OS.md 258]. Change to "85mm prime lens at f/1.4" to preserve physical logic [MASTER_VISUAL_PRODUCTION_OS.md 258].

### 4.5 QA
*   **Substandard Output Release [Fact/Principle]**: Never publish, deliver, or display any visual asset that scores below 20 on the Critique Matrix or below 85 on the 100-point QA rubric [MASTER_PROMPT_ENGINEERING_OS.md 7.11].

### 4.6 PROVENANCE
*   **Case Result Fabrication [Fact/Principle]**: Never invent, falsify, or round client results, local case studies, or regional benchmarks [MASTER_AGENT_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md 11]. All data points must be traceable to raw files [MASTER_OUTBOUND_OS.md SOP 11.1].

### 4.7 PRIVACY
*   **EEA Public-API Processing [Fact/Principle]**: Never upload or process source images, files, or videos showing recognizable real-world faces via public files/interactions APIs within EEA or UK jurisdictions [MASTER_AGENT_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md 1.2].

### 4.8 FRESHNESS
*   **Outdated Metric Usage [Fact/Principle]**: Never utilize outdated statistics, comparative figures, or market metrics older than 180 days on client landing pages [MASTER_AGENT_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md SOP 5.4].

### 4.9 PSYCHOLOGY ETHICS
*   **Desire Manufacturing [Fact/Principle]**: Never attempt to construct entirely new customer needs or manufacture market desires from scratch [MASTER_PROMPT_ENGINEERING_OS.md II.IX]. Only channel existing mass desires and frustrations directly toward the productized mechanism [MASTER_PROMPT_ENGINEERING_OS.md II.IX].

### 4.10 BUSINESS AUTHORITY
*   **Price Discounting [Fact/Principle]**: Never offer price discounts during negotiations; discounting signals desperation, destroys premium positioning, and triggers high-ticket buyer skepticism [MASTER_AGENT_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md 11]. Lower prices only when there is a commensurate reduction in scope [MASTER_AGENT_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md 11, MASTER_OUTBOUND_OS.md Card 2].
