## 3. Mindset & Operating Principles

1.  **Creative is Your Targeting:** Detailed interest targeting is dead [9]. Visual hooks, on-screen text, and native language dial in and self-qualify the demographic [14, 563]. The algorithm pushes the ad to audiences matching the profiles of users who watch past the first 3 seconds [216].
2.  **Performance Over Aesthetics ("Make Ugly Ads"):** Overdesigned, highly-polished corporate assets screen "ADVERT" and trigger immediate scrolls [246, 520, 838]. High-converting creative is low-fi, authentic, and fits organically into the consumer's social feeds (e.g., green screen talking heads, raw unedited phone footage, fake tweets) [194, 520, 898].
3.  **Speed Beats Perfection:** In performance marketing, a fast-moving, iterative system of "good-enough" assets beats a slow, high-budget, perfectionist studio production [279, 1011]. Rapid testing provides immediate market data [402, 921].
4.  **Story Over Scripting:** Audiences connect with people and narratives, not logos, brands, or lists of product features [504, 650]. High-converting ads tell stories of founding, customer struggles, or dramatic transformations [277, 504].
5.  **Data-Led Iteration (The "Lego Block" System):** Stop treating ads as fixed, monolithic assets. Deconstruct them into modular building blocks: **Hook (0-3s) + Problem + Solution + Product Demo + CTA** [189, 481]. Recombine winning blocks based on hook rate, hold rate, and CTR data [189, 190].

## 5. Core Performance Frameworks

### I. The Hero & Villain Framework
The most robust direct response script layout.
*   **The Villain:** An industry standard, a competitor, or a toxic chemical compound/habit (e.g., standard plastic razors that clog [784]; standard chewing gum made of synthetic rubber/petroleum [786]).
*   **The Aggravation:** Focus on the pain point caused by the Villain (e.g., razor burn, ingesting microplastics, losing manhood/fertility [714, 784]).
*   **The Hero:** Introduce your product as the savior (e.g., Leaf Thorn razor with cents-per-blade refills [785]; Underbrush gum using natural tree sap [786]).
*   **The Proof:** Product demo or social proof (visualizing hair/soap lather [775] or callus skin shaving off [1072]).
*   **The CTA:** Soft or hard direct response call-to-action [176].

### II. The Contrast Principle
Placing two options side-by-side to visually enforce superiority. Commonly used for "Us vs. Them" static grids.
*   **Alternative Column:** "Them" (competitor/disposable). Marked as "Rubbish", "Inferior", "Toxic", "Fills Landfills", "Chemically Loaded" [1020].
*   **Brand Column:** "Us". Marked as "Sustainable", "Clean", "Charitable", "Fits Perfectly" [1020].

### III. The Temperature Funnel Structure
*   **TOF (Cold / Unaware):** Focus on educational, non-intrusive formats. UGC selfie videos, Street Interviews, Founder origin story [276].
*   **MOF (Warm / Problem Aware):** Video Sales Letters (VSSLs), UGC Mashups/Compilations, Podcast Ads [277, 449].
*   **BOF (Hot / Most Aware):** Plain static ads, clear offer statics, testimonial grids, scarcity/urgency blocks [278, 400].

## 6. Repeatable Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

### SOP 1: Customer Insight & Review Mining
*   **Step 1:** Request a raw CSV export of all brand reviews (from Yotpo, Okendo, or Shopify) and post-purchase surveys [201, 632].
*   **Step 2:** Open a dedicated **Claude Project** or **Poppy AI Board** [121, 158]. Upload the review export, any brand guidelines, and brand URLs [121, 392].
*   **Step 3:** Use the persona prompts (see Section 7) to extract core consumer groups (e.g., pregnant moms needing morning sickness solutions [708]).
*   **Step 4:** Query the "Reddit Answers" feature or use keyword search `site:reddit.com/r/[niche]` to find unfiltered competitor complaints [631, 701]. Identify "grainy textures" or "unpleasant taste" in rival brands to turn them into an "objection-led" hook [702, 703].
*   **Step 5:** Add these validated customer language snippets into a shared **Foreplay Board** or Google Doc as the "Creative Strategy Foundation" [102, 120].

### SOP 2: AI-Assisted Static Ad Production (Canva Magic Layers Workflow)
*   **Step 1:** Generate a scroll-stopping product/lifestyle image in **Nano Banana Pro 2** via Google AI Studio or Arcads [91, 535].
    *   *Reference Technique:* Upload a flat-lay cutout of the product with transparent background as image 1 [527]. Prompt: `Make this woman sat in her bedroom at a vanity table holding the product in image 1, shot on an iPhone 17 Pro` [36, 473].
*   **Step 2:** Download the high-res generated asset and upload it to **Canva** [465].
*   **Step 3:** Open Canva's **Magic Layers** tool to automatically scan and separate the background, product, and human hand elements into editable layers [465].
*   **Step 4:** Replace low-quality AI serif text with bold, native-looking fonts (e.g., Instagram Story or TikTok Proxima Nova styles) [461, 466].
*   **Step 5:** Quickly create three variations in the same session:
    *   *Var 1:* Headline focus ("Your new go-to protein bar") [466].
    *   *Var 2:* Objection focus ("So good you'd think it wasn't healthy") [466].
    *   *Var 3:* Trust/Social Proof focus (slapping a 5-star rating + customer quote at the top) [461, 466].

### SOP 3: Meta Creative Testing Campaign Structure
*   **Campaign Objective:** Conversions (Optimized for Product Purchase) [410, 424].
*   **Targeting:** Broad (Geofenced, gender/age capped only—zero detailed interests) [410, 424].
*   **Campaign Setting:** Advantage+ or manual CBO/ABO (simplified layout: 1 Campaign, 1 Testing Ad Set per test variable) [296, 410, 424].
*   **The Testing Ad Set Layout:**
    *   Limit to **3 to 5 creatives** maximum per ad set to avoid budget dilution [411].
    *   When testing video hooks, run **4 ads** in the ad set: same body script, same CTA, but change the first 3 seconds (Hook) [412, 413].
*   **Operational Execution Gates:**
    *   **Spend Floor:** Let the ad set run until it achieves at least **50 pixel events** (purchases) or runs for a hard limit of **3 to 5 days** [413].
    *   **Data Analysis (Manual over Automatic):** Do not let Meta automatically allocate budget across an untested ad pool via dynamic creative options if you cannot see granular breakdown metrics (use manual setups to verify individual hook rates) [412].
    *   **Action Rules:** Do not kill the whole ad set. Pause losing ads inside the ad set and increase budget on the winner in place [415].

### SOP 4: UGC Casting and Vetting
*   **Step 1:** Post casting calls on **Backstage** or **Star Now** (UK) [249].
*   **Step 2:** Explicitly write a comprehensive casting brief detail:
    *   *Requirements:* The creator's age, gender, look, and skin tone **must match the buyer persona** identified during review mining (e.g., don't hire a 20yo gym-bro to sell menopause supplements) [210, 245, 918].
    *   *Vetting check:* The talent must have proven experience reading scripts naturally. Reject creators who sound like they are reading off teleprompters with flat inflections [825].
*   **Step 3:** Request **raw unedited footage** (raw MP4s) with no music or burned-in captions [79, 1005]. Use a platform like **ReCharm** to categorize and store this raw footage in a permanent asset library [518, 591].

## 8. Style, Taste, and Aesthetic Guidance

*   **Caption Styling:** Do not use overly polished, massive, flashing text across the screen [577, 1060]. Mimic the native styles of the platform being used (e.g., standard TikTok stroke text, Proxima Nova fonts, or simple Instagram story subtitles placed in native safe zones) [15, 1060, 1063].
*   **The Sound First Principle:** Sound-on viewing is dominating 2026. Audio must be clear, crisp, and high-fidelity [17]. Avoid cheap, echoey Amazon microphones; record clean raw dialogue or swap the voice using high-grade **Eleven Labs** cloned voices [80, 196, 362]. Use multi-layered sound design (e.g., real-life satisfy sounds like a knife slicing packaging [172] or a gavel slamming [135]). Keep music tracks subtle and lowfi so they don't overpower the voiceover [797].
*   **Visual Starting Frames:** The first frame of a video ad must start **mid-movement** (e.g., a hand ripping a package, pouring liquid, or reacting naturally) [772]. Never start with a blank screen, a slow transition, or a static brand logo [237].
*   **Color Blocking:** Utilize contrasting block colors (saturated orange, warning yellows, deep blacks) that clash with the default white or dark feeds of social platforms to disrupt scroll momentum [556, 557, 873].

To pass production and launch into testing campaigns, every static ad must score a **10/10** on this rubric:

*   [ ] **Structured Close:** Does the CTA clearly instruct the viewer on the next step (e.g., "Click the link below to shop the sale")? [176]

## 10. Core Failure Modes & Pitfalls

### I. The "Exploding Melon" Hook Disconnect
*   **The Trap:** Designing an outrageous, clickbait hook (e.g., a melon exploding, or a naked model on screen) to game the system and achieve a 70% hook rate [219, 348].
*   **The Cost:** Unqualified viewers click, spend zero time on the site, and bounce. Your cost-to-purchase ratio drops, CPA skyrockets, and Meta’s algorithm gets trained on the wrong signals [219, 349].
*   **The Fix:** Ensure your scroll stopper is always **conceptually relevant** to the product's pain point (e.g., crushing feet into flaky croissants to illustrate dry, flaky heels) [173, 174].

### II. Premature Creative / Campaign Killing
*   **The Trap:** Pausing an ad set or campaign after one "bad week" or a temporary dip in performance [358, 1114].
*   **The Cost:** You sacrifice evergreen scaling assets. Conversions naturally dip and rise; turning off ads disrupts Meta's learning model [358, 550].
*   **The Fix:** Give ads at least 3-5 days to optimize. If an ad has historically proven to be a winner, leave it on during brief performance fluctuations and let the algorithm self-correct [413, 550].

### III. Over-reliance on AI UGC Presets ("AI Slop")
*   **The Trap:** Churning out endless volume using generic AI talking-head avatars with unnatural lip-syncs [352, 354].
*   **The Cost:** Complete loss of consumer trust. Audiences easily identify deepfakes, leading to poor hold rates and brand fatigue [322, 352].
*   **The Fix:** Use AI UGC to rapidly test and validate scripts [134, 143]. Once validated, use AI for B-roll overlays [146] and hire real professional actors or customers to record the final, high-converting assets [80, 774].

## 13. Contradictions, Debates, & Freshness Risks

### I. The "Static vs. UGC" Launch Sequence Debate
*   *The Debate:* Some marketers argue you must start with UGC videos to build brand awareness.
*   *Fraser's Counter-Thesis:* Start with scrappy, low-cost static ads to validate copy hooks and offers [16, 404]. Once a static ad's messaging gets high CTR, roll that exact messaging out into high-cost UGC and video productions to reduce budget risk [16, 404].

### II. AI UGC Legality vs. Scaling Volume
*   *The Tension:* AI UGC platforms promise endless volume at 10x lower costs [468].
*   *The Risk (2026):* Rapidly emerging US regulatory restrictions on using AI-generated human likenesses in paid media makes AI UGC risky for long-term brand building [322]. Sticking to Claymation, 3D/Pixar-style animations, or strictly B-roll overlays is the safer operational path [301, 322].
