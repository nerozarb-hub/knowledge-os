## 2. SOURCE-BACKED OPERATING PRINCIPLES (AXIOMS)

Every successful campaign, brand asset, and B2B pipeline engineered by NEROZARB must strictly adhere to these thirteen immutable laws [NB-012, NB-013, NB-014]:

### I. Creative is the Targeting
Post-iOS14, platform delivery algorithms possess diminished demographic and interest-based tracking data [NB-004, NB-005]. Manual interest-based segmentation wastes budget and restricts optimization [NB-004, NB-005]. Leave targeting completely broad and let the platform's AI self-select the ideal customer [NB-004, NB-005]. Meta's NLP and Computer Vision subsystems (Rosetta subsystem) scan the on-screen text, audio transcripts, and video frames of your ad creative, routing it directly to users displaying congruent intent and emotional states [NB-001, NB-003]. Your ad acts as the code that programs the delivery engine [NB-003].

### II. The Hook is a Filter
The hook does not exist to entertain everyone or accumulate empty views [NB-001]. It serves as a visual and audible filter designed to capture the attention of a highly specific target demographic (the *self-selection* mechanic), weeding out unqualified scrollers to preserve conversion-rate efficiency [NB-001, NB-005]. An outrageous, clickbait hook (e.g., an exploding melon) games completion metrics but pollutes algorithmic optimization data with non-buyers, driving up CPA [NB-007, NB-012].

### III. Restraint is Authority
High-status, premium brands do not beg for attention or scream in crowded feeds [NB-008, NB-012]. They command authority through negative space, desaturated palettes, and aggressive minimalist control [NB-012]. Every element on a canvas that does not explicitly serve the strategic message is a distraction that dilutes perceived authority [NB-012]. Clutter is a signal of desperation; negative space is a signal of luxury [NB-012].

### IV. Visuals are the Cake; Words are the Icing
The human eye processes color, contrast, motion, and brightness in under 200 milliseconds (bottoms-up visual processing) [NB-012, NB-013]. This occurs long before the conscious neocortex can comprehend text or spoken audio [NB-012, NB-013]. If your visual hook does not immediately arrest the zombie-scroll in under 2.5 seconds, your world-class script is irrelevant because it will never be heard [NB-012, NB-013]. Visuals dictate the "click/stop"; words dictate the "stay" [NB-012].

### V. Dopamine is a Chemical of Prediction, Not Reward
Dopamine is released when a viewer is actively anticipating what will happen next, trying to resolve an open curiosity loop (the "Mental Itch") [NB-012]. Once the loop is closed and the reward is delivered, the dopaminergic drip stops [NB-012]. To make video narratives addictive, you must keep the brain off-balance: open a new curiosity loop *in the same breath* as closing the previous one, passing the tension baton cleanly across the timeline [NB-012].

### VI. Trust is a Mathematical Product of Content Minutes
Trust is built by compounding minutes of attention [NB-012]. You cannot convert a cold viewer into a high-ticket buyer on short-form video alone because each 30-second reel only delivers a tiny, incremental unit of trust [NB-012]. You must deploy a Barbell Content Strategy [NB-013]: use short-form to drive mass exposure ("world exposure") and long-form, newsletters, or private communities to drive deep immersion ("world immersion") off-algorithm [NB-012, NB-013].

### VII. Pain Prioritizing Trumps Opportunity Promising
The human amygdala is biologically wired to prioritize threats over opportunities to ensure survival (Loss Aversion) [NB-012]. A viewer is twice as motivated to avoid losing $10,000 as they are to make an extra $10,000 (the 2x Motivation Multiplier) [NB-012]. Copywriting and hooks that agitate a specific "Bleeding Neck" pain point will always out-convert copy that promises a vague, positive benefit [NB-012].

### VIII. Value is Comparative, Not Intrinsic
Nothing has a fixed cost [NB-013]. A product's perceived price is entirely relative to the high-ticket "anchor" shown first [NB-013]. Position your flagship, high-ticket services first to establish a high value ceiling, making your productized backend tiers feel like an absolute steal [NB-013, NB-017].

### IX. Channel Existing Mass Desire
Do not attempt to create a new need or manufacture interest from scratch [NB-013]. Locate the burning desires, frustrations, and objections already present in the target audience's mind and channel them directly toward your productized solution [NB-013].

### X. Decouple Revenue from Labor Hours
Scale is achieved only by transforming open-ended, customized services into standardized, fixed-scope "Productized Sprints" priced based on client outcome, not agency input [NB-013]. Tying revenue directly to billable hours systematically punishes efficiency and prevents business expansion [NB-013].

### XI. Sell the Vibe, Validate with Features
Intangible, aesthetic "worlds" bypass rational resistance and capture attention [NB-013]. System 1 controls 95% of purchasing decisions [NB-008, NB-009]. Use raw, emotional "vibe coding" (lighting, unpolished textures, material cues) to appeal to System 1 [NB-001, NB-013]. Use specifications and features merely as logical justifications to allow the buyer's conscious System 2 to defend their emotional purchase [NB-009, NB-013].

### XII. Optimize for Machine Ingestion Over Human Clicks
In 2026, over 40% of consumers interact with AI-powered "Answer Engines" (Perplexity, SearchGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews) weekly [NB-013]. When an AI overview block is present, traditional organic blue link CTR drops by an average of 34.5% [NB-013]. Web assets must be formatted with "Quote-Ready Syntax" and clear Schema.org entity relationships to secure AI search citations [NB-013].

### XIII. Earn Depth, Suppress Breadth
Widespread celebrity is an expensive trap [NB-013]. Achieve absolute financial sustainability by amassing a small, hyper-dedicated base of "100 to 1,000 True Fans" paying premium rates for transformative, self-interested value [NB-013].

### 4.1 The 6-Level Dopamine Ladder [NB-012]
To progress a viewer from a complete stranger on a social feed to a brand advocate, your content must guide them up these six neurological rungs [NB-012]:
1.  **Stimulation (Level 1):** Bottoms-up visual brain hacking. Triggered within 200ms using raw color contrast, high-speed motion, and lighting changes to force the optic nerve to pause the scroll [NB-012].
2.  **Captivation (Level 2):** Opening the Curiosity Loop. Triggered by presenting an open-ended, non-obvious question in their head that they must resolve (The "Mental Itch") [NB-012].
3.  **Anticipation (Level 3):** The predictive guessing game. The viewer begins guessing what the answer might be. You feed them highly structured, escalating details to fuel this prediction [NB-012].
4.  **Validation (Level 4):** Closing the loop. Delivering the non-obvious, high-value solution or shocking story climax that exceeds their expectation, releasing a massive wave of chemical relief [NB-012].
5.  **Affection (Level 5):** Shifting focus from the message to the messenger. The viewer develops affinity for the creator's raw charisma, sets, lighting, tone, and authentic visual taste [NB-012].
6.  **Revelation (Level 6):** The Pavlovian Trigger. The viewer realizes that seeing your name or face is a guaranteed shortcut to high-value solutions. Their brain releases dopamine *simply by seeing your profile icon*, cementing them as a super fan [NB-012].

### 4.2 The 4-Step Neuro-Storytelling Addiction Loop [NB-012]
Derived from the mathematics of casino game design, this loop keeps viewers locked into video narratives indefinitely [NB-012]:
1.  **The Stakes:** The "Bleeding Neck" setup [NB-012]. Establish what is at risk immediately (Time, Money, Health, or Status) [NB-012]. If there are no stakes, the brain refuses to run its predictive engines [NB-012].

3.  **The Head Fake:** Deliver an unexpected, but completely logical, twist that contradicts their initial prediction [NB-012]. This triggers a "prediction error" in the brain, causing a massive dopamine spike [NB-012].
4.  **The Rehook:** Open a brand new curiosity loop *in the same breath* as closing the previous one [NB-012]. Pass the tension baton cleanly while the viewer is still processing the head fake, keeping them locked to the screen [NB-012].

## 6. CONDITIONAL DECISION RULES

These logical, binary decision gates govern automated routing, budget adjustments, and campaign pipeline execution [NB-003, NB-004, NB-013, NB-014]:

### 6.1 Deal Value & Complexity Mapping [NB-014]
*   **IF** estimated ACV is **<\$20K (SMB / short cycle)**:
    *   **THEN** deploy BANT for rapid inbound lead filtering.
    *   **AND** pair with Sandler's upfront contracts to protect rep time.
*   **IF** estimated ACV is **\$20K - \$100K (Mid-Market)**:
    *   **THEN** deploy SPIN Selling for implication-focused discovery to progressively expand problem awareness.
*   **IF** estimated ACV is **\$100K - \$250K (High Mid-Market)**:
    *   **THEN** deploy SPICED to align the full customer bowtie (SDR $ightarrow$ AE $ightarrow$ CS) around realized business impact.
*   **IF** estimated ACV is **>\$250K+ (Strategic Enterprise)**:
    *   **THEN** standardize on MEDDPICC with rigorous stakeholder matrices to navigate procurement hurdles.

### 6.2 Campaign Budget Scaling [NB-003]
*   **IF** Campaign’s holistic Cost Per Acquisition (CAC) or cost per result is **$\le$ target threshold** over a rolling 7-day window **AND** the **CPM is $< \$20$**:
    *   **THEN** increase the daily campaign budget (CBO) by **20% to 30% once every 7 days**.
    *   **AND** initiate Horizontal Scaling by deconstructing the winning ad's triggers into new formats.
*   **ELSE** do NOT adjust budgets to prevent triggering learning resets and auction overlap.

### 6.3 Creative Fatigue & Refreshes [NB-003]
*   **IF** Campaign's cost per result spikes by **$> 25\%$ week-over-week** **OR** **CTR drops below 1.5%** **OR** **blended ROAS declines for 3 consecutive days**:
    *   **THEN** do NOT adjust manual bid caps or target audience interests.
    *   **AND** inject a new, conceptually diverse batch of creatives using the **60/30/10 Budget Allocation Rule** (60% to evergreen, 30% to conceptual tests, 10% to seasonal).
