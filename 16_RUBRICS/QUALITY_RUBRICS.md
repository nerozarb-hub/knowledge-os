## SECTION 1: SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & GROUNDING MATRIX

This document establishes the official pre-publishing quality gates and validation criteria for all NEROZARB marketing, sales, copy, and AI-generated assets. Every asset produced must be scored against its respective 10-point scale before being cleared for live deployment [NB-001, NB-011, NB-012, NB-015].

### Operational Scaling Rules (The Band Paradigm)
* **Levels 1–3 (Critical Fail / Deficient):** Contains severe anti-patterns, waxy AI plastic sheen, platform violations, or spam-triggering mechanics [NB-001, NB-014, NB-015, NB-027]. Must be deleted and sent back to production.
* **Levels 4–5 (Standard / Workable):** Factually accurate, basic compliance, but lacks diagnostic depth, unique mechanisms, or visual texture [NB-014, NB-015]. Requires mandatory strategic overlay.
* **Levels 6–7 (Operational / High Standard):** Solid execution. Passes key structural gates (such as the 3-Item Rule, safe zones, and sentence constraints), but is structurally generic [NB-001, NB-011, NB-012].
* **Levels 8–9 (Exceptional / Elite):** High value density, fully grounded in Voice of Customer (VOC) data, chiseled tactile shading, and precise psychological hooks [NB-001, NB-009, NB-027].
* **Level 10 (Mastery / Flawless / Top-Tier):** Flawless system-wide synthesis, perfect brand alignment, absolute platform compliance, and maximum conversions [NB-012, NB-015].

#### E. Traditional Loss Aversion vs. Transactional Market Heuristics [NB-014]
* **The Conflict:** Behavioral economics assumed the WTA-WTP (Willingness-to-Accept vs. Willingness-to-Pay) gap (the Endowment Effect) was driven by Loss Aversion—the emotional pain of losing an owned asset [NB-014].
* **The Resolution (Market Heuristics):** The Smitizsky, Liu, & Gneezy "Pay-to-Keep" (PTK) study proved that when owners spend money to keep an asset they already hold, their valuation matches their acquisition price (WTP), displaying no loss aversion [NB-014]. * **The Conflict:** Zoom lenses (changing focal length in-camera while static) have no equivalent in real-world human biology and feel inherently unnatural [NB-021].
* **The Resolution:** Zoom lenses can be modeled to create creeping tension/creepy unease (Kubrick style) [NB-021], but physical camera movement (dolly pushes/pulls on prime lenses) is preferred for grounded realism, intimacy, and documentary-style truth [NB-021].

#### G. The Anti-Melt SOP Image Pinning Contradiction in AI Video [NB-022]
* **The Conflict:** The Anti-Melt SOP copies the exact same image to both Start and End frames to anchor solid geometry [NB-022]. However, locking both poles of a timeline while commanding motion confuses the AI, causing it to panic and hallucinate random objects (ghost hands, floating shapes) in the middle of the clip [NB-022].
* **The Resolution:** Apply a very subtle motion slider (e.g. 0.25) and ensure the cinematic physics commands do not require massive spatial changes, keeping camera moves to a tight Z-axis slider/dolly pan [NB-022].
